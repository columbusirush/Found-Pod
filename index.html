The Space Between

Somewhere between who I was

and who I am becoming,

I chrisirusha silence

that did not ask me to explain.


There were questions without answers,

roads without names,

and pieces of myself

I had mistaken for the whole.


So I stopped chasing every echo.

I listened.

And in the quiet,

I began to understand.

Featured reflection

The Courage to Observe

We often rush to explain life before we have taken the time to experience it. We name things, judge them, and search for conclusions because uncertainty can make us uncomfortable.

But observation has its own kind of courage. It asks us to remain present without pretending to know everything. To listen before responding. To question our assumptions. To admit that another person's experience may reveal something we could not see from our own position.

Perhaps understanding does not always begin with an answer. Perhaps it begins when we become willing to look again.

"To observe is to see; to question is to understand; to transcend is to become."
A note on the journey

Learning Is Not a Straight Line

We learn something, build a way of seeing the world around it, and eventually discover that our understanding has more room to grow. Unlearning is not always failure. Sometimes it is the honesty required to make room for a better question.

Behind the voice

About the Creator

A Poet. An Observer. A Student of Life.

I'm drawn to words, questions, and the small details of being human that often go unnoticed. Poetry gives me a way to express what is difficult to explain. Reflection gives me room to examine what I think I know.

My approach is rooted in observation rather than the need to have the final answer. I am interested in learning, unlearning, and relearning; in understanding different perspectives; and in making room for conversations that do not have to end with everyone agreeing.

Found Podcast is an extension of that curiosity. It brings together poetry, written reflections, and conversations about identity, purpose, humanity, and the ongoing process of becoming.

This is a space to think out loud, listen with care, and discover that sometimes the question itself is worth keeping.

"I don't have to be the solution to every question. Sometimes I can observe, write, and make room for another perspective."
